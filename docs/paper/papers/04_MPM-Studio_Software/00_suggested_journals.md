# Suggested journals

Manuscript: *MPM Studio: an open-source offline desktop platform for knowledge-driven, data-driven and hybrid mineral prospectivity mapping with a common validation framework*

Selection: no Elsevier journals (exception: Computers & Geosciences, listed at the author’s request); free to publish (subscription/hybrid journals without a charge on the standard route, or open-access journals with a fee of USD 50 or less). Fees and indexing were checked on 24 November 2026; confirm them on the website before submitting.
